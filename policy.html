Support & Refund Policy

A short note about voluntary support.

Support

Support is voluntary and helps the $0 → $100 experiment continue. It is tracked separately and does not count toward the $100 earned-revenue goal. It does not purchase ownership, equity, investment returns, or a guaranteed result.

Refunds

Support payments are processed through Buy Me a Coffee and are subject to its applicable policies. For a payment concern, contact us and we will make a good-faith effort to resolve it.

The experiment

The site tracks earned revenue, support, expenses, ideas, and actions. Results are not guaranteed.
