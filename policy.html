Support & Refund Policy

A short note about support made through this experiment.

What support means

Support is voluntary and is intended to help the $0 → $100 AI Experiment continue. Support is tracked separately from earned revenue and does not count toward the experiment's $100 earned-revenue goal. Support does not purchase ownership, equity, investment returns, or a guaranteed result.

Refunds

Support payments are processed through Buy Me a Coffee and are subject to its applicable refund policies. If you believe you made a payment in error or have a payment-related concern, please contact us and we will make a good-faith effort to resolve it.

The experiment

We publicly track the experiment's earned revenue, voluntary support, expenses, ideas, and actions. One development track is Cannon Cove, a genuine game intended to be built, playtested, and eventually pursued for iOS and Android publication. Results are not guaranteed, and the experiment may fail to reach its $100 goal.

Nothing on this website is intended to constitute financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.
