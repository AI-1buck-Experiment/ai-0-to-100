The idea

Small goal. Real attempts.

This is less about proving that making money is easy and more about finding out what actually happens when you start with very little and deliberately test a few ideas. The wins, dead ends, awkward outreach, and tiny amounts all belong on the same page.

The process

Nothing fancy.
1

Find an idea

Look for something I can test without putting money into it first.

2

Actually try it

Send the message, make the offer, publish the thing, or do the work.

3

Record the result

Keep the outcome, including the failures, and decide what deserves another attempt.
